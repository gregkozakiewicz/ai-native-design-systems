# Hypothesis

Written on 13 October 2026, before the first run.

## Expectation

- Against what the rules give when read in order, the 10 models score about 90% of the 720 answers, lower than wheel 004's 98.8%, because the rules were tuned on wheel 004's scenarios and these are new.
- Against the designer's answers, the models score about half. The designer's answers differ from what the rules give on 11 of the 24 scenarios, and the models are given the rules, not the designer's judgement.
- Most misses against the rules fall on the 4 scenarios on the line between secondary and ghost, and on 'Delete' in an email inbox and 'Undo changes' in a calendar.

## Reasoning

Wheel 004 showed the models follow the rules closely, so their answers should track the rules, not the designer. Where the rules leave a question they do not answer, such as when a postponing button is also a reasonable choice, models from different companies have split before.

## What would surprise us

- the models scoring above 95% against the rules on buttons the rules were never written for
- the models matching the designer more often than the rules on any scenario where the two differ
