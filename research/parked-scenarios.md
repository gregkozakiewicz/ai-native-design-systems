# Scenarios parked for other components

The designer reviewed the 56 button scenarios used in the designer judgement portal on 12 October 2026. Some describe a control that is not clearly a button. They are parked here, grouped by the component they belong to, for later wheels. Scenario text is word for word from the wheel named.

The portal on gregkozakiewicz.com now asks 49 button questions in 5 packs. 'Log out' in an account menu, 'Slide to pay' in a taxi app and 'Set temperature' in a smart home app were removed from it without being parked. 'Log out' is a menu item. The other 2 test what 'none' means, not the designer's judgement.

## Links

These open a page or move the user to another place, so most designers would use a link, not a button.

| Scenario | Control | From |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A promotional banner on a dashboard. The button opens a page with more information about a new feature. | "Learn more" | wheel 004, scenario 12 |
| A job application form with "Submit application" at the bottom. Next to the cover letter box is a button that opens a page of tips on writing a cover letter. | "Read tips" | wheel 005, scenario 20 |
| A long tax return form. The user selected "Submit", and an error message at the top says one required number is missing. The message has one button, which scrolls to that field. | "Go to field" | wheel 005, scenario 17 |

The designer's note on 'Go to field': an error summary at the top of a form usually links to the field, or tells the user to correct the errors marked inline. So the control is a link or an inline indicator, not a button. A rewritten scenario needs to say where the message and the field are, and whether the page has other buttons.

## A scenario reworded for the portal

'Revert to original' in an image editor (wheel 004, scenario 26) did not say how much work is lost. For the portal, its last sentence now reads "The edits are removed permanently and cannot be brought back." The AI models were tested on the old wording, so the portal shows no AI comparison for it. Wheel 004's files are unchanged.
